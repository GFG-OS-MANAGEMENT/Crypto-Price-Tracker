# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Crypto Price Tracker project a welcoming, respectful, and positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming constructive feedback and different viewpoints.
- Providing helpful feedback and suggestions.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Communicating politely and professionally.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or disrespectful comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a collaborative environment.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions related to Crypto Price Tracker.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through the repository's available communication channels.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate discretion.
